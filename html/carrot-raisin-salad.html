Carrot Raisin Salad

Description:

This Carrot Raisin Salad is a delightful and vibrant side dish that pairs the natural sweetness of carrots and raisins with the tangy punch of crushed pineapple.

The creamy dressing, made with mayonnaise and a hint of honey and lemon juice, adds just the right amount of richness without being overwhelming.

It's the perfect balance of sweet and tangy flavors, with a hint of cinnamon for added warmth. This salad is not only delicious, but also packed with nutrients, making it a great addition to any meal.

Enjoy this refreshing and colorful salad on its own or as a side dish to your favorite main courses!

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups shredded carrots (about 4 large carrots)
  • 1 cup raisins
  • 1/2 cup crushed pineapple, drained
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon honey (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Ingredients:
    • Start by shredding the carrots. You can use a food processor or a box grater.
    • Drain the crushed pineapple to remove excess moisture.
  2. Mix the Salad:
    • In a large mixing bowl, combine the shredded carrots, raisins, and crushed pineapple.
  3. Make the Dressing:
    • In a small bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, honey (if using), lemon juice, salt, and cinnamon (if using).
  4. Combine and Chill:
    • Pour the dressing over the carrot mixture and toss until well combined.
    • Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
  5. Serve:
    • Give the salad a final stir and serve chilled.

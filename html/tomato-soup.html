Tomato Soup

Description:

This Classic Tomato Soup is a heartwarming and flavorful dish that's perfect for any time of the year. The sweetness of the tomatoes is balanced by the savory notes of the onion and garlic, while the vegetable broth adds depth to the flavor.

A touch of sugar enhances the natural sweetness of the tomatoes, and the optional red pepper flakes provide a subtle kick. Blending the soup creates a velvety texture, and adding heavy cream makes it extra luxurious and creamy.

Fresh basil leaves add a burst of color and fresh aroma, making this soup both visually appealing and irresistibly delicious. Serve it with a side of crusty bread or a grilled cheese sandwich for the ultimate comfort meal.

Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cans (28 ounces each) whole peeled tomatoes
  • 2 cups vegetable broth or chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional, for a bit of heat)
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream (optional, for a creamier soup)
  • fresh basil leaves, chopped (for garnish)

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Ingredients:
    • Chop the onion and mince the garlic.
    • Open the cans of whole peeled tomatoes and set aside.
  2. Cook the Base:
    • In a large pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat.
    • Add the chopped onion and cook until softened, about 5-7 minutes.
    • Add the minced garlic and cook for another 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add Tomatoes and Broth:
    • Add the whole peeled tomatoes (with their juice) to the pot, breaking them up with a spoon.
    • Pour in the vegetable broth and stir to combine.
  4. Season and Simmer:
    • Add the sugar, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes (if using).
    • Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer for about 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Blend the Soup:
    • Use an immersion blender to purée the soup until smooth. Alternatively, you can carefully transfer the soup in batches to a blender and blend until smooth. Be cautious with hot liquids.
    • Return the soup to the pot if using a blender.
  6. Add Cream (Optional):
    • If you prefer a creamier soup, stir in the heavy cream and heat through without boiling.
  7. Serve:
    • Ladle the soup into bowls and garnish with fresh basil leaves.
